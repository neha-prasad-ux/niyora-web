# Content.md

The running content plan for marketing Niyora. A living doc. Add raw ideas at the bottom, promote them up into a pillar when they firm up.

## How to use this

- Three pillars: **Blog/SEO** (ranks on Google, top of funnel), **Research notes** (short, cited, builds trust), **Founder/build-in-public** (your voice, LinkedIn + X).
- The recycle loop: write one piece deep, then spin it into a LinkedIn post and an X thread that link back.
- Voice: quiet, evidence-led, founder-spoken. No hype. No em dashes. No emojis in body copy.
- Your edge runs through everything: trained doctor (Indian system of medicine), growth designer, privacy-first founder. Few people can write from all three.

Status key: `idea` -> `drafting` -> `published`.
